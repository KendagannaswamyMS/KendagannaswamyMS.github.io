About

An engineer who ships systems and studies data

I have been with JSS Science and Technology University since 2016. I joined as System Analyst at SJCE, which is part of JSSSTU, and in February 2022 I became Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, where I also lead the University IT Team. Before JSSSTU I worked as a back-end developer at Wiredelta. I hold an M.Tech from JSSSTU, a B.E. from ATME College of Engineering, Mysuru (VTU), and a diploma from JSS Polytechnic, Mysuru.

With the IT Team, I designed and built JEMS, the JSS Education Management System. It is one platform that runs admissions, academics, examinations, research, HR, finance and campus operations, with a companion Android app for staff.

My research applies machine learning and deep learning to flood risk in Kerala: multi-criteria decision analysis, MaxEnt and frequency-ratio ensembles, U-Net models on AI embeddings, and socioeconomic vulnerability. I also work on multimodal sentiment analysis and applied generative AI.

Flagship build

JEMS: JSS Education Management System

An in-house platform that brings every part of running the university into one integrated system, so data entered once is used everywhere: from a student's admission to their exam results, and from a faculty member's leave request to its approval.

AdmissionsAcademicsExaminationsResearchHRFinanceCampus operations

The Android app adds leave applications with live balances, half-day leave and document upload, in-app approvals, a searchable department directory, and faculty achievements and news on the home screen.
